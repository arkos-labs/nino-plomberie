# Content Quality & E-E-A-T Analysis

**Score: 72/100**  
**Status:** Solid foundation with growth opportunities

---

## Executive Summary

The site demonstrates **strong trust signals** (real reviews, named artisan, local address, clear guarantees) but lacks **depth of expertise demonstration**. Content explains the business well for conversion, but lacks the technical depth and educational content needed for competitive SEO ranking.

**Key Gap:** Service pages explain WHAT is done but not HOW or WHY at a technical level.

## Critical Gaps (What Needs Work)

### 🔴 Limited Depth on Service Explanation

**Issue:** Service pages (e.g., fuite-d-eau) explain WHAT but not HOW or WHY  
**Example:** Water leak page says "We detect and repair quickly" but doesn't explain:
- Detection methods (tracer gas? thermal imaging? pressure testing?)
- Common causes of leaks
- Prevention strategies
- Diagnostic process steps
- Why certain materials fail

**Impact:** Readers don't understand expertise depth. Lost opportunity to rank for informational queries ("How to find hidden water leaks").

**Recommendation:** Expand service pages from 100-150 words to 300-500 words with:
```
Service: Réparation Fuite d'eau
├── How We Detect (diagnosis methodology)
├── Common Causes (educate reader)
├── Prevention Tips (add value)
├── Timeline & Process (set expectations)
└── Materials & Standards (technical credibility)
```

### 🟡 Thin Content on Individual Service Pages

**Issue:** Service pages have minimal main content—mostly feature lists  
**Pages affected:** débouchage, robinetterie, chauffage, etc.

**Metrics:**
- Homepage: ~800 words (good)
- Service pages: 100-150 words (thin)
- About page: ~400 words (okay)

**Recommendation:** Add 200-400 more words per service page with:
- Problem explanation section
- Process/methodology
- Common mistakes section
- Related services
- FAQ specific to that service

### 🟡 No Blog or Thought Leadership Content

**Issue:** No blog, guides, or regularly updated content  
**Impact:** No freshness signals. No evidence of ongoing expertise.

**Recommendation:** Create 5-10 blog articles like:
- "Top 5 Winter Plumbing Problems" (seasonal, 600 words)
- "Water Leak Warning Signs" (evergreen, 500 words)
- "Choosing Water Heater Types" (buyer's guide, 800 words)
- "Bathroom Renovation Timeline" (detailed guide, 1000 words)
- "DIY Fixes vs. When to Call a Plumber" (educational, 600 words)

**Why:** Demonstrates current expertise. Ranks for long-tail keywords. Gives potential customers reasons to return.

### 🟡 Limited Technical Credentials Displayed

**Issue:** While 20+ years stated, no mention of certifications  
**Missing:** Professional standards, qualification details, certifications

**Current:** Just "20 ans experience"  
**Better:** "20 ans d'expérience en plomberie/chauffage. Artisan qualifié conforme aux normes NF. Intervention garantie 2 ans."

**Recommendation:** If applicable, add:
- Qualifications professionnelles (plumber certifications)
- RGE status (renewable energy qualification, if applicable)
- Professional association memberships
- Insurance information
- Standards compliance (NF, DTU, etc.)

### 🟠 Realisations Gallery Lacks Technical Detail

**Issue:** Before/after photos are beautiful but minimal explanation  
**Current:** "Douche à l'italienne avec verrière noire et baignoire"  
**Better:** "Douche à l'italienne carrée noir mat, paroi coulissante sans profilé (60x90cm), plomberie masquée sous chape, mitigeur thermostatique Hansgrohe, système de douche escamotable. Réalisé en 5 jours, finition carrelage par artisan spécialisé."

**Impact:** Photos show skill but don't demonstrate the complexity or expertise required.

**Recommendation:** For each major project in realisations:
- Add 2-3 sentence description
- Mention specific challenges
- Note materials used (brands if high-end)
- Timeline
- Technical decisions made

### 🟠 FAQ Could Demonstrate More Expertise

**Issue:** FAQs are useful but basic—"What should I do if..." not "Why does this happen"  

**Current Style:** "Coupez l'arrivée d'eau"  
**Expert Style:** "Pourquoi l'eau fuit ? [Explique les causes techniques] → Que faire ? [Solutions]"

**Recommendation:** Restructure FAQs to be educational:
- "Why does a radiator go cold at the bottom?" (Explains sludge, bleeding, thermostat function)
- "Why do burst pipes happen in winter?" (Explains freeze mechanism, prevention)
- "Why choose two artisans for bathroom?" (Explains coordination benefit, quality)
