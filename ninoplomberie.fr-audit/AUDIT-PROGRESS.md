### 🔴 Critical Issues Identified
1. **NO JSON-LD Schema Markup** - Homepage has NO structured data
   - Missing LocalBusiness schema (impacts local search visibility)
   - Missing Organization schema
   - Missing Service schema (for individual service pages)
   - Missing FAQPage schema (site has FAQ section)
   - Missing AggregateRating schema (blocks rich snippets for reviews)
   - **Impact:** Significantly reduces visibility in Google Local, AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Bing Copilot)

2. **AI Search Visibility** - Without schema, AI systems cannot reliably cite business info
   - No structured data for bots to extract verified facts
   - All bots are allowed (good) but site data is not machine-readable
