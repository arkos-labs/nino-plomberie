## Key Findings

   - Strong local keyword targeting ("Plombier Muret", "Plombier Toulouse")
   - Comprehensive LocalBusiness schema with ratings, hours, service area
   - Google Business Profile CID properly integrated (9239381501337303445)
   - 4.4/5 rating with 78 real reviews (strong social proof)
   - Clear service area definition (205 communes listed)

2. **Solid Technical SEO (82/100)**
   - HTTPS fully enabled, proper redirects
   - Fast page load times (~466ms)
   - Excellent mobile responsiveness
   - Comprehensive XML sitemap with 50+ pages
   - robots.txt allows all crawlers including AI bots
   - No console errors or broken resources
   - Proper schema markup foundation (WebSite, LocalBusiness, FAQPage)

3. **Good Mobile Experience**
   - Tested at 375px viewport — fully responsive
   - No horizontal scroll issues
   - Touch targets appropriately sized
   - Professional, clean design

4. **Business Identity Clear & Trustworthy**
   - Named artisan (not a platform)
   - Verifiable location, phone, email
   - Real testimonials from customers
   - 2-year guarantee clearly stated
   - 24/7 availability emphasized
   - Transparent pricing policy

---

### 🟡 Opportunities (Priority Improvements)

1. **Missing Service-Level Schema (75/100 Schema Score)**
   - Individual service pages (`/services/fuite-d-eau`, etc.) lack dedicated Service schema
   - **Impact:** Blocks visibility for "plombier fuite d'eau toulouse" type queries
   - **Fix:** Add Service schema to 7 service pages (2-3 hours)
   - **Urgency:** HIGH — Do in Week 1

2. **Thin Service Page Content (72/100 Content Score)**
   - Service pages: 100-150 words (too short)
   - Missing: Technical depth, "how we diagnose" explanations, prevention tips
   - **Impact:** Ranks for brand queries, not informational queries
   - **Fix:** Expand each page to 300-500 words (10 hours for all)
   - **Urgency:** HIGH — Do in Weeks 2-3

3. **No Blog / Thought Leadership Content**
   - Site has only static pages (no blog)
   - Missing freshness signal
   - Missing long-tail keyword opportunities
   - **Impact:** Limited organic traffic growth, no ongoing authority building
   - **Fix:** Create 5-10 initial blog articles, then monthly cadence (30 hours)
   - **Urgency:** MEDIUM — Do in Month 2

4. **Minor Technical Polish Needed**
   - Canonical tags point to non-www (site is www) — minor inconsistency
   - No visible breadcrumb navigation (schema exists but not visual)
   - OG:image is logo instead of preview image
   - Email address not displayed on contact page
   - **Impact:** Low (minor optimization opportunities)
   - **Fix:** Various small changes (5 hours total)
   - **Urgency:** MEDIUM — Do in Week 1
